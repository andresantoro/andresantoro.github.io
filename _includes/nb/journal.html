Entry · {{ site.time | date: "%B %Y" }}

We set out from London with a notebook full of networks, spent three winters by the lake in Geneva, then crossed the Alps and made camp in Turin, at the ISI Foundation.

The quarry has not changed. Pairs never told us the whole story, so we keep listening for the triplets: the synergy hidden in brain activity and market signals, where three voices say what no two can say alone.

Part of the party sails with Project CETI, charting who talks to whom among sperm whales, one coda at a time. Another keeps the stove lit, mapping how the world’s cuisines pair their ingredients.

Supplies: an MSCA fellowship for temporalHOI, and a grant to study tinnitus. Morale: high. Reviewer 2: still at large, as pictured above.

The camp is growing, and new recruits are welcome: the open projects board says where to start.
